Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

OnlinePDFPro (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides browser-based document tools and a small library of paid study materials. This policy explains what is processed locally, what leaves your device, and which providers receive data when a feature requires them.

1. Information we collect

Accounts and purchases

If you create an account, Supabase Auth receives and stores your email address, account profile, authentication data, and (if you use Google sign-in) the name, email address, and profile photo supplied by Google. For study-material purchases, we store order identifiers, product identifiers, payment references, entitlement status, and the account connected to the purchase. Razorpay processes your payment details; OnlinePDFPro does not receive or store your card, UPI, bank, or wallet credentials.

Usage and technical data

Our hosting, CDN, security, and analytics providers may process IP address, browser/device information, request timestamps, and basic usage events. We use this information for security, reliability, measurement, and support. We do not sell personal information.

2. What happens to your files

Tools that are labelled local read the file in your browser and do not upload it. Browser processing still creates temporary in-memory or local browser data while the page is open. Do not rely on a generic “never leaves your device” statement: each tool page identifies exceptions.

  • Local tools: PDF editing, merging, splitting, password protection, unlock, image conversion (JPG/PNG/WebP), and the default English OCR run in the browser unless the page explicitly says otherwise.
  • AI tools: Chat with PDF, summarization, flashcards, and vision features send the text or images you submit through our Cloudflare Worker to the configured AI provider (Groq and/or OpenRouter). Do not upload confidential material unless you accept that processing.
  • PDF↔Word: the selected document is sent through our protected Cloudflare Worker gateway to an isolated Modal conversion service. Conversion providers may temporarily process the document while the job runs; review the tool notice and output before relying on it.
  • OCR cloud retry: image-to-text uses local Tesseract first. OCR.space receives a document only after you check the explicit cloud-consent box. The selected language is sent with the file.
  • QR file modes: Text/URL mode does not upload a file. Image, Any File, Folder, and Video modes upload the selected content to the provider you choose: tmpfiles.org produces a link intended to expire after about 60 minutes; an ImgBB key sends the image to ImgBB, whose hosting and retention policy may be permanent. These links are public to anyone who has them.

3. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare — DNS/CDN, static hosting, Worker API gateway, Turnstile anti-abuse verification, rate limiting, and security telemetry.
  • GitHub / GitHub Pages — source control and static website delivery. Public repository content is visible according to GitHub settings.
  • Supabase — authentication, account sessions, database records, and purchase entitlements.
  • Razorpay — checkout and payment processing. Razorpay may process payment and fraud-prevention data under its terms.
  • Modal — isolated server-side PDF-to-Word and Word-to-PDF conversion when you use those tools.
  • OCR.space — optional cloud OCR retry only after explicit consent.
  • tmpfiles.org — temporary file hosting for QR file, folder, and video destinations when selected.
  • ImgBB — optional image hosting for QR image destinations when you provide an ImgBB API key.
  • Groq and OpenRouter — AI inference providers used by the Worker for text, vision, summarization, flashcards, and chat features. The active provider can vary when fallback routing is used.
  • Google — OAuth sign-in (only when you choose Google sign-in) and fonts/measurement services loaded by some pages.

Each provider has its own terms and privacy policy. The provider list and tool disclosures may be updated when our architecture changes.

4. Cookies, storage, and service worker

We use browser storage for theme preference, recent-tool/history data, cart state, and Supabase authentication session data. The site may register a service worker to cache static assets and provide an offline shell. Clear site data in your browser to remove local storage and cached assets. Analytics and security providers may set or read their own cookies where enabled by the page.

5. Retention and deletion

Local files and generated outputs remain under your control unless you upload them to an external provider. External providers apply their own retention, deletion, and abuse-monitoring rules. Account and purchase records are retained while needed to provide the service and meet legal, tax, dispute, and security requirements. Contact us if you need account deletion or a data-access request.

6. Security

We use HTTPS, a Content Security Policy, server-side payment verification, authentication checks, rate limiting, and bounded conversion/OCR requests. No internet transmission or third-party service is completely risk-free. Keep passwords and private documents out of public QR links and do not share conversion or AI outputs containing sensitive data.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Use local tools and leave the cloud OCR consent box unchecked to keep processing in your browser.
  • Do not provide an ImgBB key or use QR file modes when you do not want external hosting.
  • Choose whether to create an account or use account-free local tools.
  • Request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent by contacting support@onlinepdfpro.com.

8. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features, providers, or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current revision. Questions or requests can be sent to support@onlinepdfpro.com.
