Privacy Policy

How OnlinePDFPro protects your files with local in-browser processing, zero mandatory accounts, and clear opt-in controls. Last updated: November 2026.

1. Zero Accounts & Free Access

OnlinePDFPro is built to work immediately without collecting personal profiles. You do not need to register an account, sign in, or submit payment details to use our 46+ PDF tools or to view and download our free college study PDFs.

2. How Your Documents Are Processed

We design our tools so that your files stay on your device whenever possible, and we clearly disclose when a specialized feature requires an external service:

  • 100% Local Browser Tools: Core utilities—including Merge PDF, Split PDF, Compress PDF, PDF Editor, Sign PDF, Rotate PDF, Password Protect/Unlock PDF, and Image Converters—process files directly inside your browser’s memory using WebAssembly and HTML5 Canvas. Your files are never uploaded to our servers.
  • PDF ↔ Word Conversion: When you use PDF to Word or Word to PDF, the selected file is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS through our Cloudflare Worker gateway to an isolated Modal conversion container solely to perform the format conversion and return your output file.
  • AI & Cloud OCR Features (Consent-Gated): Image-to-Text runs locally via Tesseract.js by default and only contacts OCR.space if you explicitly check the cloud retry box. AI tools (Chat with PDF, PDF Summarizer, and Flashcards) send extracted document text through our Worker gateway to Groq/OpenRouter only after you provide explicit consent.
  • QR Code File Modes: Standard text/URL QR codes are generated 100% locally. Optional file/image QR hosting uploads the chosen asset to tmpfiles.org (~60-minute temporary link) or ImgBB (when you supply an API key).

3. Free College Study Materials

When you view or download our free college study guides (such as the 51-page DBMS Complete Notes PDF), the PDF is served directly to your browser as a static file. We do not track individual student reading histories or require any personal information.

4. Local Storage, Service Worker & Analytics Choices

  • Local Browser Storage: We use localStorage strictly on your device to remember your Light/Dark theme preference, optional local tool history, and your privacy banner choice. You can clear this anytime via your browser settings.
  • Offline Service Worker: Our Service Worker caches static page assets so core tools continue to load quickly even on unstable connections.
  • Analytics Control: Cloudflare Web Analytics collects anonymous, aggregate performance metrics without cookies. Optional Google Analytics loads only if you explicitly click “Allow analytics” in the privacy banner, and can be disabled at any time via “Privacy choices” in the footer.

5. Security & Contact

All traffic to OnlinePDFPro is protected by HTTPS encryption, strict Content-Security-Policy (CSP) headers, and Cloudflare Turnstile anti-abuse protection. We never sell or rent user data.

For any privacy questions or data requests, email us at support@onlinepdfpro.com.
