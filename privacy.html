Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

OnlinePDFPro ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Full name
  • Profile photo (if you sign in with Google)

Purchase Information

When you purchase study materials, we store:

  • Order details and payment references
  • Product purchase history
  • Purchase entitlement records used to provide library access

Automatically Collected Information

We may collect basic analytics data such as page views, browser type, and general usage patterns to improve our services.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process purchases and deliver digital products
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve our tools and services
  • To communicate important service updates

3. Authentication

We use Supabase Auth for account management. You can sign up with email and password or use Google OAuth for one-click login. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google. We do not receive or store your Google password.

4. PDF Tool Processing

Most of our PDF tools process your files entirely in your browser — your documents never leave your device. Some tools that require server-side processing (such as AI-powered tools like Chat with PDF, PDF Summarizer, and cloud conversion) send your data to external processing APIs. This is clearly noted on each tool's page.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare — Hosting, CDN, and security
  • GitHub Pages — Website hosting
  • Supabase — Authentication and database
  • Google — OAuth sign-in
  • Razorpay — Secure checkout and payment processing
  • AI processing APIs — For AI-powered PDF tools (only when you use those specific tools)

Each service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

Payments are processed by Razorpay. OnlinePDFPro does not store your card, UPI, banking, or other payment credentials; we receive only payment and order references needed to confirm your purchase and provide access.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage for:

  • Theme preference (light/dark mode)
  • Shopping cart data
  • Authentication session tokens (managed by Supabase)

We do not use third-party tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

7. Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data will be removed. Purchase records may be retained for legal and accounting purposes.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, secure authentication, and Content Security Policy headers. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@onlinepdfpro.com.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will indicate the date of the latest revision at the top of this page.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@onlinepdfpro.com.
