Dealing with multiple PDF documents can be a hassle, especially when you need to share them via email or present them as a unified report. Merging PDF files into a single, cohesive document is the best way to organize your files, save time, and ensure your recipient gets exactly what they need in one click.

## Why Merge PDFs?

- **Better Organization:** Keep related documents together. Combine invoices, receipts, or project reports into one neat file.
- **Easier Sharing:** Email clients often have attachment limits. One consolidated PDF is easier to attach and download than 10 separate files.
- **Professional Presentation:** Submitting a single document looks much more professional than sending a scattered folder of individual pages.
