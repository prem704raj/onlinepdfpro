Whether you need to submit a digital portfolio, share a scanned document, or create an album to send to family, turning a collection of scattered image files into a single, cohesive PDF is the way to go.

PDFs are universally supported, meaning your images will look the same regardless of what device or software the recipient is using. In this post, we'll cover how to quickly transform JPG, PNG, or GIF images into a polished PDF document.

## Why Convert Images to PDF?

- **Consolidation:** Combine 20 individual photos into one single file, making it much easier to share over email or chat.
- **Standardization:** Ensure your document can be opened and viewed exactly as intended on any device.
- **Protection:** PDFs can be secured with passwords and encryption, providing a layer of security that raw images lack.
